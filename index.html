The centre Navi key is the only general-purpose button — its function is the single word shown at the bottom-centre of the screen (e.g. "Menu", "Open", "Call").
The C key and the scroll rocker are fixed-function: C implicitly clears / backs out, the rocker scrolls — neither ever gets an on-screen label.
The demo drives itself — touch any key or press a mapped key and the tour AND rotation stop for good: you're driving.
